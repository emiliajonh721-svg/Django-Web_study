English for Specific Purposes

20 words

Itinerary

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A planned route or schedule of a journey.

Example:

Please check your itinerary so you know what time the bus leaves tomorrow.

Landmark

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A famous or easily recognized building or place.

Example:

Angkor Wat is the most famous landmark in Cambodia.

Commentary

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

Spoken information or explanations given during a tour.

Example:

Feel free to ask questions during my commentary as we walk through the temple.

Brochure

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A small booklet containing pictures and information about a place.

Example:

I will hand out a brochure with a map of the museum before we enter.

Admission

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The fee paid to enter a place, or permission to enter.

Example:

Your admission ticket to the national park is already included in the tour price.

Souvenir

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

Something bought or kept as a reminder of a place visited.

Example:

We will stop at the local market so you can buy a souvenir for your family.

Heritage

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

Traditions, monuments, and culture passed down from the past.

Example:

This ancient city is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Scenic

Part of speech: Adjective

Meaning

Having beautiful natural views.

Example:

We are taking the scenic route along the river to reach the waterfall.

Depart

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To leave a place to start a journey.

Example:

Our boat will depart from the pier at exactly 8:00 AM.

Disembark

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To leave a ship, aircraft, or bus at the end of a journey.

Example:

Please watch your step as you disembark from the ferry.

Excursions

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

Short trips or outings taken for pleasure, usually during a longer vacation.

Example:

We offer optional afternoon excursions to the floating village.

Punctual

Part of speech: Adjective

Meaning

Happening or doing something at the agreed or proper time.

Example:

Please be punctual when returning to the bus so we stay on schedule.

Hospitality

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The friendly and generous reception and entertainment of guests.

Example:

Local villagers are well known for their warm hospitality.

Navigate

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To find the right way to get to a place.

Example:

The old town has narrow streets, so follow me closely while I navigate.

Refreshments

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

Light snacks and drinks provided to guests.

Example:

Cold water and refreshments will be served after our walking tour.

Etiquette

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The customary code of polite behavior in a society or specific place.

Example:

Proper temple etiquette requires covering your shoulders and knees.

Guided

Part of speech: Adjective

Meaning

Led or accompanied by someone who shows the way and explains things.

Example:

After our guided tour of the palace, you will have one hour of free time.

Assemble

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To gather together in one place as a group.

Example:

Let’s assemble near the main gate in twenty minutes.

Architecture

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The style and design of buildings.

Example:

Notice the intricate stone carvings and ancient architecture of this shrine.

Accommodate

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To provide space for, or to help with someone's needs.

Example:

Let me know if you have any dietary restrictions so we can accommodate you at lunch.

Behind the Profession

More Than Just Showing the Way

A great tour guide is a storyteller, a cultural ambassador, and a problem-solver. Whether you are guiding travelers through ancient temple ruins, bustling local markets, or scenic riverbanks, your words bring history and traditions to life.

What Happens on a Real Tour?

  • • The Welcome: Setting a warm, friendly first impression at the airport or hotel lobby.
  • • The Journey: Explaining routes, safety rules, and keeping everyone punctual.
  • • The Storytelling: Sharing fascinating facts about heritage, architecture, and local etiquette.
  • • The Care: Accommodating guest needs, recommending food, and handling surprises calmly.

Golden Rule of Guiding

"Tourists may forget the exact dates of a monument, but they will always remember how welcomed, safe, and inspired you made them feel."
