20 words

Itinerary

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A planned route or schedule of a journey.

Example:

Please check your itinerary so you know what time the bus leaves tomorrow.

Revenue

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The total amount of money a company earns from its normal business activities before expenses.

Example:

Our company's annual revenue grew by twenty percent after launching the new product.

Negotiate

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To discuss terms formally in order to reach a mutually acceptable agreement.

Example:

Both companies met today to negotiate the final price of the supply contract.

Stakeholder

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

Any person, group, or organization that has an interest in or is affected by a business.

Example:

We present a quarterly progress report to keep all investors and stakeholders informed.

Agenda

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A structured list of items and topics to be discussed during a formal meeting.

Example:

The first item on today's meeting agenda is reviewing our marketing budget.

Deadline

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The latest time or date by which a task or project must be completed.

Example:

Our team worked extra hours to submit the client proposal before Friday's deadline.

Budget

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A financial plan that estimates expected income and limits spending over a set period.

Example:

We managed to finish the office renovation well within our approved budget.

Delegate

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To assign a task or responsibility to another team member, usually someone in a junior role.

Example:

Effective managers know how to delegate daily tasks so they can focus on long-term goals.

Profitable

Part of speech: Adjective

Meaning

Generating more financial income than expenses; making a net profit.

Example:

The export division became highly profitable after expanding into Southeast Asian markets.

Proposal

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A formal written plan or offer submitted to a client or manager for approval.

Example:

The board of directors approved her proposal to open a new branch office.

Benchmark

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A standard or point of reference against which business performance or quality is measured.

Example:

Last year's customer satisfaction score serves as our benchmark for this quarter.

Outsource

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To hire an outside company or specialist to handle specific business tasks or services.

Example:

Many startups outsource their accounting and payroll to save time and reduce costs.

Merger

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

The legal combining of two separate companies into a single larger organization.

Example:

The merger between the two regional banks created the largest financial group in the country.

Leverage

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To use existing resources, skills, or advantages to achieve the best possible business result.

Example:

We can leverage our strong social media presence to attract international customers.

Inventory

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A complete list or stock of goods and materials a business currently holds for sale.

Example:

Our warehouse team checks the inventory every Friday to restock popular items.

Quarterly

Part of speech: Adjective / Adverb

Meaning

Happening or published once every three months (four times in a financial year).

Example:

All department heads meet for a quarterly review to evaluate sales targets.

Liability

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A company's legal financial debts or obligations owed to others.

Example:

Paying off short-term loans helped reduce the company's total financial liability.

Incentive

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A reward or bonus offered to motivate employees to work harder or customers to buy.

Example:

The company offers year-end cash bonuses as an incentive for top sales performers.

Streamline

Part of speech: Verb

Meaning

To make a business process simpler, faster, and more efficient by removing unnecessary steps.

Example:

Switching to digital invoices helped streamline our client billing process.

Subsidiary

Part of speech: Noun

Meaning

A smaller company that is owned and controlled by a larger parent corporation.

Example:

The telecommunications group opened a local subsidiary in Phnom Penh last year.

Inside the Corporate World

More Than Just Office Talk

Business English is the global language of trade, entrepreneurship, and leadership. Mastering professional vocabulary empowers you to lead meetings, negotiate clear agreements, and collaborate smoothly with international partners.

What Happens in Modern Business?

  • • The Strategy: Setting clear agendas, analyzing benchmarks, and scaling operations.
  • • The Finance: Tracking revenue, managing budgets, and increasing profit margins.
  • • The Negotiation: Presenting proposals, reaching consensus, and closing contracts.
  • • The Teamwork: Delegating tasks, meeting deadlines, and updating stakeholders.

Golden Rule of Business English

"Clarity builds trust—when you communicate your ideas with precision and respect, doors open in every industry."
